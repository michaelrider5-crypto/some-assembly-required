Meet Michael

Hi, I'm Michael.

After retiring, I started Some Assembly Required because I genuinely enjoy putting things together and helping people.

Whether you've purchased furniture for your home, office, garage, or a new bicycle, I'd be happy to assemble it so you can spend your time enjoying it instead of sorting through bags of hardware and instruction booklets.

If I can save you a few hours of frustration, then I've done my job.
